## How to study Social Science

- **Learn the lists:** the 7 continents, the 5 oceans, the 3 levels of Panchayati Raj. Say them aloud until you can say them without thinking.
- **Use a map.** Look at a world map and a map of India for 10 minutes a day. Point to things as you name them.
- **Use your own surroundings.** Is your village or town on a plain, a plateau, near a river? Who in your area does a primary, secondary or tertiary job?
- **Make small cards.** A question on the front and the answer on the back. Flip through them on the bus, at lunch or before bed.
- **Write short answers in your own words.** In a 3-mark answer, aim for three clear points.
- **Check your textbook.** For things where books differ (like how many oceans there are), follow what your school textbook says.
