## The big idea

There are millions of kinds of living things on Earth, from tiny insects to huge whales. This huge variety is called **diversity**. To make sense of it, scientists sort living things into groups. That sorting is called **classification**.

## Living and non-living

Living things:
- need **food** and **air**
- **grow**
- **respond** to their surroundings (a plant bends towards light, you pull your hand from a hot pan)
- **move** (animals move around, plants move too, slowly)
- make **waste** and get rid of it
- **reproduce** (make more of their own kind)
- eventually **die**

Non-living things don't do all of these. A car moves, but it doesn't grow, eat or reproduce. A dry seed looks dead, but when it gets water it grows, so it's alive.

## Habitat and adaptation

A **habitat** is the place where a living thing lives: a forest, desert, pond or ocean. Habitats are mostly **terrestrial** (on land) or **aquatic** (in water).

An **adaptation** is a feature of a living thing's body or behaviour that helps it survive in its habitat.

| Living thing | Habitat | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Camel | desert | stores fat in its hump, wide feet that don't sink in sand, loses very little water |
| Cactus | desert | thick stem stores water, leaves are spines to cut down water loss |
| Fish | water | streamlined body, fins to swim, gills to breathe in water |
| Polar bear | very cold lands | thick fur, a layer of fat, white colour |
| Duck | ponds | webbed feet for swimming |
| Eagle | air, mountains | sharp beak and claws, strong wings |
| Lotus | ponds | floating leaves, hollow stem |

## Plants

Sorted by size and kind of stem:

| Group | What they're like | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Herbs** | small, soft, green stems | grass, wheat, mint, coriander |
| **Shrubs** | medium, many woody branches from near the base | rose, hibiscus |
| **Trees** | tall, with a hard woody trunk and branches high up | mango, neem, banyan |
| **Creepers** | weak stems that spread along the ground | pumpkin, watermelon |
| **Climbers** | weak stems that climb up a support | pea, money plant |

## Animals

The biggest split: does the animal have a **backbone**?

- **Vertebrates** have a backbone.
- **Invertebrates** don't (insects, worms, snails, spiders, jellyfish, starfish).

**The five groups of vertebrates**

| Group | Skin covering | How they breathe | Young | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fish** | scales | gills (in water) | lay eggs in water | rohu, shark |
| **Amphibians** | smooth, moist skin | gills when young, lungs (and skin) when adult | lay eggs in water | frog, toad |
| **Reptiles** | dry scales | lungs | lay eggs on land | snake, lizard, crocodile, turtle |
| **Birds** | feathers | lungs | lay eggs | crow, hen, eagle |
| **Mammals** | hair or fur | lungs | give birth, feed milk | cow, human, whale, bat |

- **Warm-blooded** animals (birds and mammals) keep a steady body temperature. The rest are **cold-blooded**.
- Whales and dolphins live in water, but they're mammals, not fish. They breathe air and feed their babies milk.
- Bats fly, but they're mammals, not birds. They have fur and feed milk.
- **Insects** are invertebrates with **6 legs** and a body in 3 parts (head, thorax, abdomen). **Spiders** have **8 legs**, so they aren't insects.
- Mushrooms are **not** plants. They belong to a separate group, the fungi.

## Memory tips

- **Vertebrate = Vertebra = backbone.** Both start with "vert".
- **FARBM** for the five vertebrates: **F**ish, **A**mphibians, **R**eptiles, **B**irds, **M**ammals. (Say "Fabulous Animals Run, Bounce, Move".)
- **Amphibian = "double life".** *Amphi* means both: life in water and on land.
- **Hair means mammal. Feathers mean bird. Scales mean fish or reptile.** Wet and slimy scales: fish. Dry scales: reptile.
- **Insect has six letters, and six legs.** Spiders belong to a different group (arachnids) and have eight legs.
- **MRS GREN** is a classic way to remember the seven signs of life: **M**ovement, **R**espiration, **S**ensitivity, **G**rowth, **R**eproduction, **E**xcretion, **N**utrition.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Saying a whale is a fish. It's a mammal.
- Calling a spider an insect. It has 8 legs.
- Saying plants don't move. They do, just slowly (a sunflower follows the Sun).
- Forgetting that habitat means *where* an animal lives, while adaptation means *how* its body helps it live there.
- Treating mushrooms as plants.

## Quick card

- Living: food and air, growth, response, movement, waste, reproduction, death
- Plants: herbs, shrubs, trees, creepers, climbers
- Vertebrates: fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds, mammals
- Insects: 6 legs. Spiders: 8 legs.
- Habitat = where it lives. Adaptation = how its body helps it live there.
