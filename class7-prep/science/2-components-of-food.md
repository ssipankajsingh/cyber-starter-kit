## The big idea

Food gives us energy, helps us grow, and keeps us healthy. Different foods contain different **nutrients** (the useful substances in food). There are 5 main nutrients, plus roughage and water.

## The nutrients

| Nutrient | What it does | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| **Carbohydrates** | give energy | rice, wheat, potato, sugar, bread |
| **Fats** | give energy (more than carbohydrates do, for the same weight) | ghee, butter, oil, nuts |
| **Proteins** | help us grow, and repair the body ("body-building") | pulses (dal), milk, eggs, fish, meat, soyabean |
| **Vitamins** | protect the body and keep it working | fruits, vegetables, milk, sunlight (for vitamin D) |
| **Minerals** | build bones and blood, help the body work | milk, green vegetables, salt, fish |
| **Roughage** (fibre) | helps food move through the body | whole grains, fruits, vegetables |
| **Water** | helps digest food, carries nutrients, removes waste | water, fruits, milk |

**A handy way to group them**
- **Energy-giving:** carbohydrates and fats
- **Body-building:** proteins
- **Protective:** vitamins and minerals

## Vitamins and minerals, and what happens without them

Not getting enough of a nutrient causes a **deficiency disease**.

| Missing | Disease or problem | Good sources |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | slow growth, weak body (kwashiorkor) | pulses, milk, eggs |
| Vitamin A | night blindness (trouble seeing in dim light) | carrot, papaya, milk, eggs |
| Vitamin B1 | beriberi (weak muscles) | whole grains, pulses |
| Vitamin C | scurvy (bleeding gums, slow healing) | amla, lemon, orange, guava |
| Vitamin D | rickets (soft, bent bones) | sunlight, milk, eggs, fish |
| Calcium | weak bones and teeth | milk, curd, green vegetables |
| Iron | anaemia (tiredness, too little blood) | spinach, jaggery, meat |
| Iodine | goitre (swelling in the neck) | iodised salt, seafood |

## Testing food in the lab

| Test for | You add | Result if present |
|---|---|---|
| Starch (carbohydrate) | a drop of dilute **iodine** solution | turns **blue-black** |
| Protein | a few drops of **copper sulphate** and **caustic soda** | turns **violet** |
| Fat | rub the food on **paper** | leaves an **oily patch** that lets light through |

## A balanced diet

A **balanced diet** has all the nutrients in the right amounts: cereals for energy, pulses and milk for protein, fruits and vegetables for vitamins, minerals and roughage, and plenty of water. Eating too little is bad, and so is eating too much of one thing.

**Cooking tips that save nutrients**
- Don't wash rice and dal too many times. You wash the nutrients away.
- Don't overcook vegetables. Heat destroys some vitamins.
- Wash vegetables **before** you cut them, not after.

## Memory tips

- **Three jobs:** *Energy* (carbs and fats), *Build* (protein), *Protect* (vitamins, minerals). "Carbs fuel, Protein builds, Vitamins guard."
- **Sunshine = vitamin D.** Rickets is **R** for **R**ickety bones.
- **C for Citrus, C for scurvy.** Amla and oranges stop it.
- **A for Awake at night.** Vitamin A prevents night blindness.
- **Iodine in salt, goitre in the neck.** *Iodine fights goitre.*
- **Iodine test turns starch blue-black.** Think "I-odine, I make Ink".

## Mistakes to avoid

- Saying proteins give the main energy. Carbohydrates are the main energy source, and proteins are for growth.
- Mixing up vitamin deficiencies. Use the table and the tips above.
- Forgetting water and roughage. They aren't nutrients that feed us, but we need them.
- Thinking one food has everything. That's why a *balanced* diet matters.

## Practice (do these on paper)

1. Name the two nutrients that give us energy.
2. Which nutrient is called "body-building"?
3. Name a deficiency disease caused by a lack of (a) vitamin C (b) vitamin D (c) iodine (d) iron.
4. What happens to a food if it contains starch and you add iodine?
5. Which test shows that a food has protein?
6. How do you test for fat in a food?
7. Which vitamin do we get from sunlight?
8. Why is roughage important, even though it gives no nutrients?
9. What is a balanced diet?
10. Why shouldn't you wash vegetables after cutting them?
11. Name two foods rich in calcium.
12. A child has trouble seeing in dim light. Which vitamin is missing, and which foods would help?
