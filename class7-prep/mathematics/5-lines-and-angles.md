## The big idea

An **angle** is the amount two lines open up where they meet, like a door opening or a pair of scissors. We measure angles in **degrees** (°). One full turn is 360°.

## Types of angles

| Name | Size |
|---|---|
| Acute | less than 90° |
| Right | exactly 90° |
| Obtuse | more than 90° and less than 180° |
| Straight | exactly 180° |
| Reflex | more than 180° and less than 360° |
| Complete turn | 360° |

## Pairs of angles

- **Complementary angles** add up to **90°**. The complement of 65° is 90° − 65° = **25°**.
- **Supplementary angles** add up to **180°**. The supplement of 110° is 180° − 110° = **70°**.
- **Adjacent angles** share a corner and a side, and sit side by side.
- A **linear pair** is two adjacent angles that sit on a straight line, so they add up to **180°**.
- **Vertically opposite angles** are the angles directly across from each other when two lines cross. They are always **equal**.

When two straight lines cross, you get four angles. The ones opposite each other are equal, and the ones next to each other add up to 180°.

## Parallel lines and a transversal

**Parallel lines** never meet, like railway tracks. A line that cuts across them is a **transversal**. It makes 8 angles, and they come in pairs:

| Pair | What's true |
|---|---|
| Corresponding angles (same position at each crossing) | **equal** |
| Alternate interior angles (inside, on opposite sides) | **equal** |
| Co-interior angles (inside, on the same side) | add up to **180°** |

## Angles in a triangle

The three angles of any triangle add up to **180°**.

## Solved examples

**1. Find the complement of 65° and the supplement of 110°.**
Complement = 90 − 65 = **25°**. Supplement = 180 − 110 = **70°**.

**2. A linear pair has angles x and 4x. Find both.**
They add up to 180°: x + 4x = 180, so 5x = 180, so x = 36. The angles are **36° and 144°**.

**3. Two lines cross and one angle is 50°. Find the other three.**
The angle opposite is also **50°**. The two angles beside it are each 180 − 50 = **130°**.

**4. Two angles of a triangle are 50° and 60°. Find the third.**
180 − 50 − 60 = **70°**.

## Memory tips

- **"C for Corner, S for Straight."** Complementary makes a **C**orner (90°). **S**upplementary makes a **S**traight line (180°).
- **"Opposites are twins."** Vertically opposite angles are equal. The ones next to each other are a linear pair and add to 180°.
- **The letters trick.** Parallel lines with a transversal make a shape like a Z, an F or a C. The **Z** (alternate) angles are equal, the **F** (corresponding) angles are equal, and the **C** (co-interior) angles add to 180°.
- **The 90 and 180 split.** For a complement, subtract from 90. For a supplement, subtract from 180. Say it out loud: "C is for 90, S is for 180".

## Mistakes to avoid

- Mixing up complement (90°) and supplement (180°).
- Saying co-interior angles are equal. They **add up to 180°**.
- Forgetting that a triangle's angles add up to 180°, not 360°.
- Measuring from the wrong zero on a protractor. Check whether your angle is smaller or bigger than 90° first, then pick the matching scale.

## Practice (do these on paper)

1. Find the complement of 40°.
2. Find the supplement of 75°.
3. Two complementary angles are x and (x + 20°). Find both.
4. Two supplementary angles are in the ratio 2 : 3. Find them.
5. Two straight lines cross and one angle is 35°. Find the other three angles.
6. A linear pair is made of x and 2x. Find x.
7. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One of a pair of corresponding angles is 70°. What is the other?
8. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One co-interior angle is 80°. What is the other?
9. Two angles of a triangle are 40° and 70°. Find the third angle.
10. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3 o'clock?

<summary>Show the answers (try first!)</summary>

1. 90 − 40 = 50°
2. 180 − 75 = 105°
3. x + x + 20 = 90, so 2x = 70, so x = 35. The angles are **35° and 55°**.
4. The 5 parts add to 180°, so 1 part = 36°. The angles are 2 × 36 = **72°** and 3 × 36 = **108°**.
5. Opposite angle = 35°. The other two = 180 − 35 = **145°** each.
6. x + 2x = 180, so x = 60 (and the other angle is 120°).
7. 70° (corresponding angles are equal)
8. 180 − 80 = 100°
9. 180 − 40 − 70 = 70°
10. 90°

## Formula card

- Complementary: sum = 90°
- Supplementary and linear pair: sum = 180°
- Vertically opposite angles: equal
- Corresponding angles and alternate interior angles: equal
- Co-interior angles: sum = 180°
- Angles of a triangle: sum = 180°
