## Nouns: the naming words

A **noun** is the name of a person, place, thing or idea.

| Kind | What it names | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Common | any ordinary person, place or thing | boy, city, dog |
| Proper | one particular name (starts with a capital letter) | Riya, Delhi, Monday |
| Collective | a group, seen as one | team, herd, flock, class, bunch |
| Abstract | a feeling or idea you can't touch | honesty, beauty, kindness, childhood |
| Material | what something is made of | gold, wood, milk, cotton |

**Groups with the right name:** a herd of cows, a flock of sheep, a pack of wolves, a swarm of bees, a bunch of keys, a team of players, a fleet of ships.

## Making plurals

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| Most nouns: add **-s** | book → books, pen → pens |
| Ends in s, x, z, ch, sh: add **-es** | bus → buses, box → boxes, watch → watches, dish → dishes |
| Consonant + y: change y to **-ies** | baby → babies, city → cities |
| Vowel + y: just add -s | boy → boys, key → keys |
| Many words ending -f or -fe: change to **-ves** | leaf → leaves, knife → knives, wife → wives |
| Some -o words add -es | potato → potatoes, tomato → tomatoes |
| Some -o words add only -s | photo → photos, piano → pianos |
| Irregular | man → men, woman → women, child → children, tooth → teeth, foot → feet, mouse → mice |
| Same in singular and plural | sheep, deer, fish |

## Pronouns: the replacing words

A **pronoun** takes the place of a noun, so you don't repeat it. *Riya lost Riya's bag* sounds silly. *Riya lost her bag* is better.

| Person | Subject (does it) | Object (receives it) | Belongs, before a noun | Belongs, on its own | Itself |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | you | your | yours | yourself |
| he | he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | it | its | — | itself |
| we | we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

Examples: **She** called **me**. This is **my** book. This book is **mine**. He hurt **himself**.

**Other pronouns**

- **Demonstrative:** this, that, these, those (*This is mine.*)
- **Question:** who (the person doing it), whom (the person receiving it), whose (belonging to), which, what
- **Relative:** who (people), which (things), that (people or things). They join two parts of a sentence: *The girl **who** won the prize is my friend.*
- **Indefinite:** someone, anyone, everybody, nobody, something, nothing

## Memory tips

- **Subject does, object gets.** *He (subject) helped me (object).* If it comes before the verb, it's usually a subject word. After the verb, usually an object word.
- **Put yourself last.** It's polite and correct: *Riya and **I** went*, not *I and Riya went*.
- **Its has no apostrophe** when it shows belonging: *The dog wagged its tail.* *It's* is short for "it is".
- **Abstract nouns are the ones you can't photograph.** You can't take a picture of honesty.

## Mistakes to avoid

- *Me and my friend went to the park.* Say **My friend and I went.**
- *The childs, mans, foots.* Those are irregular: **children, men, feet**.
- *This book is my.* Use **mine** when no noun follows.
- *Who did you see?* In careful writing it's **Whom** did you see, because the person is the object.

## Practice (do these on paper)

1. Name the kind of noun: (a) Delhi (b) honesty (c) herd (d) gold (e) teacher.
2. Write the plural: (a) baby (b) box (c) leaf (d) child (e) boy (f) tooth.
3. Fill in the collective noun: a ___ of bees; a ___ of sheep.
4. Fill in with a pronoun: Riya lost ___ bag. (his / her)
5. This pen belongs to me. It is ___. (my / mine)
6. Priya is clever, and everybody likes ___. (she / her)
7. The boys enjoyed ___ at the picnic. (themselves / theirselves)
8. The girl ___ won the race is my cousin. (who / which)
9. ___ book is this? It is not mine. (Who / Whose)
10. Rewrite correctly: *Me and Ravi went to the market.*
11. The cat licked ___ paws. (it's / its)
12. Replace the nouns with pronouns: *Ravi gave the book to Meena, and Meena thanked Ravi.*

## Quick card

- Noun kinds: common, proper, collective, abstract, material
- Plurals: -s, -es, -ies, -ves, and irregulars (men, children, teeth, feet, mice)
- Subject: I, he, she, we, they. Object: me, him, her, us, them
- my / mine, your / yours, her / hers: use the second kind when no noun follows
- *its* = belonging, *it's* = it is
