## The big idea

- A **synonym** is a word with the **same (or nearly the same) meaning**: *big* and *large*.
- An **antonym** is a word with the **opposite meaning**: *big* and *small*.

This part of the paper is about your **word power**. The only real way to build it is to learn words regularly. A few every day beats a long list the night before.

## Making antonyms with prefixes

Many opposites are made by adding a small piece at the front:

| Prefix | Examples |
|---|---|
| un- | happy → unhappy, kind → unkind, fair → unfair |
| dis- | agree → disagree, honest → dishonest, appear → disappear |
| in- | visible → invisible, complete → incomplete, correct → incorrect |
| im- (before p or m) | possible → impossible, polite → impolite, patient → impatient |
| il- (before l) | legal → illegal, logical → illogical |
| ir- (before r) | regular → irregular, responsible → irresponsible |
| mis- | understand → misunderstand, behave → misbehave |

It doesn't work for every word. The opposite of *strong* isn't *unstrong*. It's **weak**.

## Memory tips

- **Learn in pairs.** Write the word and its opposite on the same line. Your brain stores them together.
- **Picture it.** For *expand / contract*, imagine a balloon blowing up and then going flat.
- **Synonyms aren't perfectly identical.** *Big, huge* and *enormous* all mean large, but each is bigger than the last. In the exam, choose the closest match.
- **One word, several opposites.** The opposite of *light* is **heavy** (about weight) but **dark** (about colour). Look at the sentence to decide.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Mixing up the two. Synonym = **same**. Antonym = **opposite**. (*Antonym* has "anti" in it, like "against".)
- Using the wrong prefix: *unpossible, dispolite*. Check the table above.
- Picking a word that is related but not the same. *Hot* and *warm* are related, but the opposite of *hot* is **cold**.

## Quick card

- Synonym = same. Antonym = opposite.
- Prefixes: un-, dis-, in-, im- (p, m), il- (l), ir- (r), mis-
- Learn words in pairs. A few a day beats a lot in one night.
