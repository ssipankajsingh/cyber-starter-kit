## The big idea

A **preposition** is a small word that shows how a noun or pronoun is connected to the rest of the sentence: where something is, when it happens, or how.

*The book is **on** the table. We meet **at** 5 o'clock. She came **by** bus.*

Prepositions are tricky because there often isn't a rule, just the way English is used. But the common ones follow patterns.

## Time: at, on, in

| Use | For | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | exact time, and a few set phrases | at 5 o'clock, at noon, at night, at midnight |
| **on** | days and dates | on Monday, on 15 August, on my birthday |
| **in** | months, years, seasons, parts of the day | in July, in 2025, in summer, in the morning |

**Other time words**

- **since** + a starting point: *since 9 o'clock, since Monday, since 2020*
- **for** + a length of time: *for two hours, for five years*
- **by** means "not later than": *Finish by 5 o'clock.*
- **during** means "within a period": *during the holidays*
- **before / after**: *before lunch, after school*
- **till / until**: up to a point: *Wait till 6.*

## Place: at, in, on and friends

| Word | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | a point | at the bus stop, at school |
| **in** | inside something | in the box, in India |
| **on** | on a surface | on the table, on the wall |
| **under / below** | lower than | under the chair |
| **above / over** | higher than | the lamp is above the table |
| **between** | two things | between the bank and the school |
| **among** | many things | among the trees |
| **beside / next to** | at the side | beside me |
| **behind / in front of** | back / front | behind the door |
| **near / opposite** | close / facing | near the park |

## Direction

**to** (towards a place: *go to school*), **into** (entering: *jumped into the pool*), **from** (starting point), **through** (inside and out the other side: *through the tunnel*), **across** (from one side to the other: *across the road*), **along**, **up**, **down**, **towards**.

## Words that go together

Many words need one particular preposition after them. Learn these as pairs:

| good **at** | afraid **of** | interested **in** |
|---|---|---|
| fond **of** | proud **of** | angry **with** (a person) |
| depend **on** | listen **to** | wait **for** |
| laugh **at** | believe **in** | different **from** |
| full **of** | married **to** | sorry **for** |

## Memory tips

- **At = a point, On = a surface, In = inside.** A dot, a line, a box.
- **Days and dates take On. Months and years take In. Clock times take At.** *On Monday, in July, at 5.*
- **Since = the start. For = how long.** *Since Monday* (start), *for three days* (length).
- **Between is for two, among is for many.** Think of "TWo" and "between": both have a T and a W sound.
- **Learn the pairs as one lump.** Don't think "afraid, then something". Think "afraid of".

## Mistakes to avoid

- *on July*, *in Monday*. Use **in July**, **on Monday**.
- *I have lived here since five years.* Say **for five years**.
- *She is good in singing.* Say **good at**.
- *He is angry on me.* Say **angry with me**.
- *The ball is between the three boys.* With more than two, use **among**.

## Practice (do these on paper)

Fill in the blank with the right preposition.

1. The class starts ___ 8 o'clock.
2. My birthday is ___ 12 March.
3. We go on holiday ___ summer.
4. I have been waiting here ___ two hours.
5. She has lived in Kurukshetra ___ 2019.
6. The cat is hiding ___ the sofa. (below it)
7. The book is ___ the table. (on the surface)
8. The ball rolled ___ the road to the other side.
9. Share the sweets ___ the four children.
10. The school is ___ the bank and the post office.
11. She is afraid ___ the dark.
12. He is good ___ maths.
13. Please listen ___ your teacher.
14. We travelled to Delhi ___ train.
