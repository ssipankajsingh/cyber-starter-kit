## The big idea

Picture a rectangular field.

- The **perimeter** is the distance all the way **around** the edge. It's how much fence you'd need.
- The **area** is the space **inside**. It's how much grass you'd need to cover it.

| English | हिन्दी |
|---|---|
| perimeter | परिमाप |
| area | क्षेत्रफल |
| length, breadth | लंबाई, चौड़ाई |
| radius | त्रिज्या |
| circumference | परिधि |

Perimeter is measured in plain units (cm, m). Area is measured in **square** units (cm², m²), because you're counting little squares.

## The formulas

| Shape | Perimeter | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Rectangle (length l, breadth b) | 2 × (l + b) | l × b |
| Square (side a) | 4 × a | a × a |
| Triangle (base b, height h) | add the 3 sides | ½ × b × h |
| Parallelogram (base b, height h) | 2 × (sum of two neighbouring sides) | b × h |
| Circle (radius r) | 2 × π × r (called the circumference) | π × r × r |

For π, use **22/7** (when the radius is a multiple of 7) or **3.14**. The question will usually tell you which.

**Height** in a triangle or parallelogram means the straight-up distance between the base and the top, a right angle to the base. It is **not** the slanted side.

## Units you might need to convert

- 1 m = 100 cm, so 1 m² = 100 × 100 = **10,000 cm²**
- 1 km = 1000 m

Always change everything to the **same unit** before you calculate.

## Solved examples

**1. A rectangle is 12 cm long and 5 cm wide. Find its perimeter and area.**
Perimeter = 2 × (12 + 5) = 2 × 17 = **34 cm**.
Area = 12 × 5 = **60 cm²**.

**2. A triangle has base 10 cm and height 6 cm. Find its area.**
Area = ½ × 10 × 6 = **30 cm²**.

**3. A circle has radius 7 cm. Find its circumference and area (π = 22/7).**
Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = **44 cm**.
Area = 22/7 × 7 × 7 = 22 × 7 = **154 cm²**.

**4. A rectangular field is 50 m by 30 m. Fencing costs ₹20 per metre. What does it cost to fence the whole field?**
Perimeter = 2 × (50 + 30) = 160 m. Cost = 160 × 20 = **₹3,200**.
(Fencing goes around the edge, so it's perimeter, not area.)

## Memory tips

- **"Area is the carpet, perimeter is the fence."** Carpet covers the floor, so square units. Fence goes round the edge, so plain units.
- **Triangle = half a rectangle.** Two matching triangles make a rectangle (or parallelogram), so a triangle's area is half of b × h. That's the ½.
- **Circle: "Round the edge, 2πr. Fill it in, πr²."** The one with the little 2 on top (r²) is the area, because area has the *squared* units.
- **Reading a word problem:** "fence", "border", "boundary", "wire around", "walk once round" mean perimeter. "Cover", "paint", "tile", "carpet", "grass" mean area.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Forgetting the **½** for a triangle.
- Using the slanted side instead of the straight-up **height**.
- Writing cm instead of cm² for an area.
- Mixing units (say metres and centimetres) in one calculation.
- Mixing up radius and diameter. The diameter is **twice** the radius, so halve it first if the question gives the diameter.

## Formula card

- Rectangle: P = 2(l + b), A = l × b
- Square: P = 4a, A = a²
- Triangle: A = ½ × b × h
- Parallelogram: A = b × h
- Circle: C = 2πr, A = πr²
- 1 m² = 10,000 cm²
