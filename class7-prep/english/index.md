## How to get better at grammar fast

- **Read aloud.** Your ear often knows the right answer before your brain does. "She go to school" sounds wrong. Trust that feeling, then find out why.
- **Read English every day.** A story, a newspaper page, a comic. Anything. Correct grammar sinks in just from seeing it.
- **Learn the signal words.** Words like *yesterday*, *since*, *already* and *between* point straight at the right answer. Each chapter lists them.
- **Cross out the wrong options.** In a multiple-choice question, two answers are usually clearly wrong. Remove them first.
- **Keep a "mistakes list".** Write each wrong answer and the correct one. Read it the night before the exam.
- **Learn 5 new words a day** for synonyms and antonyms. Write each with its opposite.
