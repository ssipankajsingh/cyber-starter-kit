## When you've finished

You've built four real security tools. Not many people your age can say that. Next steps if you want more:

- Try a beginner security platform such as TryHackMe (it has a free section), and see what you enjoy most.
- Look up "bug bounty" to see how people get paid for finding problems with permission.
- Ask your school or a local club whether they run a cybersecurity or coding group, or start one.
- Pick the job from the tables in each project that sounded most interesting, and search for "a day in the life of" it.
