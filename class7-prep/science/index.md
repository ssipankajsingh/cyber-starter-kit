## How to study Science

- **Know the "why".** Don't just learn that the clinical thermometer has a kink. Learn *why* it has one. Examiners love "why" questions.
- **Learn tables as tables.** Food and deficiency diseases, animal groups: write a blank table and fill it from memory.
- **Draw what you can.** A quick sketch of a thermometer, a sieve or a frog's life cycle helps you remember it, even if you never draw it in the exam.
- **Use real life.** Sieving flour, drinking water, a fan spinning. Every topic here is something you can see at home today.
- **Practise the sums.** Speed and temperature conversion are easy marks, but only if you've practised.
- **Keep a one-page summary** per topic, and read it on the day before the exam.
