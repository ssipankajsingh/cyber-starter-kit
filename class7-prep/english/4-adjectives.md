## The big idea

An **adjective** describes a noun or pronoun. It tells you what kind, how many, or which one.

*a **tall** boy, **three** apples, **this** book, **my** bag*

## Kinds of adjective

| Kind | Tells you | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Quality | what kind | a **brave** soldier, a **sweet** mango, **red** roses |
| Quantity | how much | **some** water, **enough** food, **little** time |
| Number | how many | **five** pens, **many** boys, **second** floor |
| Demonstrative | which one | **this** pen, **those** shoes |
| Possessive | whose | **my** bag, **their** house |
| Question | asking | **Which** book? **Whose** pen? **What** colour? |

## Degrees of comparison

Adjectives change when you compare things. There are three steps:

- **Positive:** just describes: *Ravi is tall.*
- **Comparative:** compares **two** things (use *than*): *Ravi is **taller than** Amit.*
- **Superlative:** the top of **three or more** (use *the*): *Ravi is **the tallest** boy in class.*

**How to make them**

| Rule | Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short word: add -er / -est | tall | taller | tallest |
| Ends in e: add -r / -st | large | larger | largest |
| One vowel + one consonant: double the last letter | big | bigger | biggest |
| Ends in consonant + y: change y to i | happy | happier | happiest |
| Long words (3+ syllables): use more / most | beautiful | more beautiful | most beautiful |
| Irregular: learn by heart | good | better | best |
| | bad | worse | worst |
| | many / much | more | most |
| | little | less | least |
| | far | farther / further | farthest / furthest |
| | old | older (elder for family) | oldest (eldest) |

**Equal comparison:** use *as ... as*. *Meena is **as** tall **as** Riya.*

## Memory tips

- **"Than" goes with two, "the" goes with the top.** *Taller than*, *the tallest*.
- **Good, better, best. Bad, worse, worst.** Say it as a chant until it's automatic.
- **Short words take -er, long words take more.** *Taller*, but *more beautiful*. (Never both: not *more taller*.)
- **Spot the adjective:** ask "what kind?", "how many?" or "which?" about a noun. The answer is the adjective.

## Mistakes to avoid

- *more taller*, *most tallest*. Use one way only: **taller**, **tallest**.
- *gooder*, *badder*. Say **better** and **worse**.
- *Ravi is the taller of the three.* With three or more, use the superlative: **the tallest**.

## Practice (do these on paper)

1. Name the adjective: *She wore a beautiful red dress.*
2. Name the kind of adjective in **three** apples.
3. Give the comparative: (a) tall (b) big (c) happy (d) beautiful
4. Give the superlative: (a) large (b) good (c) bad (d) much
5. Fill in: Ravi is ___ than Amit. (tall)
6. Fill in: Mount Everest is ___ mountain in the world. (high)
7. Fill in: This bag is ___ than that one. (heavy)
8. Fill in: She is the ___ girl in the class. (clever)
9. Fix the mistake: *My sister is more taller than me.*
10. Fix the mistake: *This is the most good book I've read.*
11. Fill in: Meena is as ___ as Riya. (smart)
12. Pick the right word: He ran ___ than all the others. (far / farther / farthest)

## Quick card

- Adjective: describes a noun
- Comparative = two things + *than*. Superlative = three or more + *the*
- big → bigger → biggest. happy → happier → happiest
- good → better → best. bad → worse → worst
- Never use *more* and *-er* together
