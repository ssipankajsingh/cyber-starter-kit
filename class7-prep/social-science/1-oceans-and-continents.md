## The big idea

Seen from space, Earth looks blue, because water covers about **71%** of its surface and land covers only about **29%**. That's why Earth is sometimes called the **blue planet** or the **water planet**.

The huge pieces of land are **continents**. The huge areas of salt water are **oceans**.

## The 7 continents

From **largest to smallest**:

1. **Asia** (the largest, and the one where India is)
2. **Africa**
3. **North America**
4. **South America**
5. **Antarctica** (covered in ice, almost no people live there permanently)
6. **Europe**
7. **Australia** (also called Oceania) (the smallest)

## The oceans

From **largest to smallest**:

1. **Pacific Ocean**: the largest and deepest. Its deepest spot, the **Mariana Trench**, is about 11 km down.
2. **Atlantic Ocean**
3. **Indian Ocean**: the only ocean named after a country (India)
4. **Southern Ocean**: around Antarctica
5. **Arctic Ocean**: the smallest, around the North Pole, mostly frozen

**A note on counting oceans.** Most books today list **five** oceans, as above. Some older books list four (they leave out the Southern Ocean). If your school textbook says four, learn it that way for your exam.

## Handy facts

- **Asia** is the biggest continent, and has the highest mountain, **Mount Everest** (about 8,849 m).
- **Africa** and **South America** are crossed by the **equator**.
- **Antarctica** is the coldest continent and sits around the **South Pole**.
- **Australia** is the smallest continent.
- The **equator** is an imaginary line round the middle of Earth. It splits Earth into the **Northern Hemisphere** and the **Southern Hemisphere**.
- Oceans are salty. The Pacific alone is bigger than all the land put together.

## Memory tips

- **Continents, big to small: "All Animals Need Sleep, And Eat Anything."** The first letters give you **A**sia, **A**frica, **N**orth America, **S**outh America, **A**ntarctica, **E**urope, **A**ustralia, in that order.
- **Oceans, big to small: "Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic" = P.A.I.S.A.** Say it like "paisa" (money).
- **Australia is smallest, Asia is biggest.** Start and end of the list.
- **Pacific means "peaceful".** It was named that by an explorer who found it calm.
- **Points to a world map** whenever you say a name. Your eyes remember too.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Mixing up the order of the continents. Practise saying the list.
- Thinking Antarctica is the smallest continent. It's fifth, and Australia is smallest.
- Calling Australia "just a country". It's a country and a continent.
- Forgetting that the Pacific is the biggest and the Arctic is the smallest.

## Practice (do these on paper)

1. What percent of Earth is covered by water?
2. Name the seven continents.
3. Which is the largest continent? Which is the smallest?
4. Name the five oceans, from largest to smallest.
5. Which ocean is named after a country?
6. Which ocean is the deepest? What is its deepest part called?
7. On which continent is India?
8. Which two continents does the equator pass through?
9. Which continent is covered in ice?
10. What is the equator?
